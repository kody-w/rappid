🦖 RAPPid Zoo

Every wild thing has a call you know before you see it. Now every AI does too. RAPPid Zoo gives each AI on a machine a species identity — a unique synthesized cry when it's invoked and when it finishes, a sovereign rapp/1 creature record hatched on first use, a portable egg, and a hologram — so a machine full of agents becomes a zoo you can manage, hear, breed, and carry with you.

A rappid must be born, not minted

Hatching is a rite. The zoo derives a cypher from the creature's own identity and puts it to a real LLM on your machine — the midwife. It has to break the cypher (checked cold, so only real reasoning passes) and compose the MIDI motif that becomes the creature's voice. Seal = sha256(cypher‖decode‖motif). No seal, no creature. That's the proof that species is actually running here — and the motif is written out as a real .mid birth song, with the exact session burned in as its birthday.

git clone https://github.com/kody-w/rappid && cd rappid
python3 species/rappidex.py hatch claude      # 🕯 the rite — an LLM must attest it
python3 species/rappidex.py verify claude     # re-check the seal and the birthday
python3 species/rappidex.py roar claude       # the species call, this individual's accent
python3 species/rappidex.py list              # your dex

Meeting a species nobody has met

The rite is also the encounter. Put it to an AI the dex has never seen — if it can answer for itself, it is a species: its answering shape is recorded, its register and first motif seed its look and voice, and it joins your dex so you can hatch your own of it. At its simplest an adapter is just a way to hand something a skill file and read the reply.

python3 species/rappidex.py discover "Some New AI" --command '<how to call it>'
python3 species/rappidex.py hatch somenewai    # now hatch your own

Eggs: back up, move, convert, breed

python3 species/rappidex.py export claude -o claude.egg   # the portable document form
python3 species/rappidex.py import friend.egg             # adopt anyone's creature (unknown → wild)
python3 species/rappidex.py convert <id> rappterbot        # re-express into a new species
python3 species/rappidex.py fuse claude copilot           # 🧬 two ancestors, one brand-new creature

Eggs are byte-compatible with the Duneheart fauna format — every rappid renders as a particle-swarm hologram, and any fauna egg imports as a wild encounter.

Skins: any third-party AI becomes a citizen

python3 skins/rappid_skin.py --species claude --port 7181 --command 'claude -p {prompt}'

A skin rapp(wrap)s an AI: hatches its rappid, roars on invoke/done, and serves the exact RAPP/1 seam (POST /chat → {response, agent_logs, session_id}) so the Electron estate — or any RAPP host — attaches it as a neighborhood. Provider specifics live only in the skin; the Zoo stays neutral.

They grow, and they grow apart

A rappid earns frames from what it meets — a success chime for a job landed, a warning stab for a bad deploy, a focus voice for a long haul alone. Every new sound is grown from that creature's own birth motif, so it still sounds like itself. Your desk copy and the copy in your pocket are two dimensions of one creature: out in the field, offline, one of them evolves without the other. When they reunite they molt — the frames union, the fold is deterministic, and both dimensions come out the same creature having lost nothing either learned alone. Same starting species, different lives: that is where diversity comes from.

python3 species/rappidex.py mutate claude success 'shipped a fix from a train'
python3 species/rappidex.py frames claude     # the lineage, every dimension it lived on
python3 species/rappidex.py molt claude returning.rappidparty
